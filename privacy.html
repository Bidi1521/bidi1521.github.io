Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-07

Content Machine ("the app") is a personal, single-operator automation tool used to generate and publish short-form video content to YouTube and TikTok on behalf of its sole operator.

What data we access

Content Machine connects to your YouTube and TikTok accounts via OAuth to upload videos on your behalf. We only request the minimum scopes needed to upload video content (e.g. video.publish, video.upload for TikTok; YouTube Data API upload scope for YouTube).

What we do with it

Access tokens are stored locally on the operator's own machine and are used solely to publish video content that the operator has generated and manually approved. No data is shared with, or sold to, any third party. No data is used for advertising or analytics purposes beyond basic upload status.

Data retention

Tokens and generated content are retained locally for as long as the operator continues to use the app, and can be deleted at any time by disconnecting the account or removing local files.
